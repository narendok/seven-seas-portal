## 1. Evaluation Methodology & Scoring Scale
- **Rating 1 (Learner)**: Understands theory; requires constant guidance.
- **Rating 2 (Supervised)**: Executes routine tasks under periodic supervision.
- **Rating 3 (Independent)**: Works independently with full problem-solving competency.
- **Rating 4 (Expert / Trainer)**: Master competency; capable of training others and auditing processes.
- **Classification Levels**: Level 1 (<25%), Level 2 (26%–50%), Level 3 (51%–75%), Level 4 (76%–100%).
- **Rule**: Any score $< 3.0$ in mandatory domain skills triggers automatic **Training Need Identification (TNI)**.
